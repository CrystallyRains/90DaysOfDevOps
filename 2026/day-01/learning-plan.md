# My 90-Day DevOps Learning Plan

> *This document outlines my goals and learning plan for the next 90 days as I work towards strengthening my DevOps skills through consistent learning, hands-on projects, and continuous improvement.*

---

## Current Level

I am currently a working professional. In my current role, I work on cloud projects, create learning content around AI and cloud technologies, and build video tutorials on AI agents and AI tools.

While this has given me a solid foundation in cloud technologies, I now want to deepen my DevOps knowledge and gain more hands-on experience with production-oriented tools, workflows, and engineering practices.

---

## Why I'm Doing This

Over the past few months, I've been learning and working with cloud technologies, but I want to build a stronger foundation in DevOps and become more confident in applying these concepts to real-world projects.

This challenge is an opportunity to stay consistent, build practical projects, improve my interview readiness, and prepare for my next career move into a DevOps role with better learning and growth opportunities.

---

## Goals for the Next 90 Days

- Strengthen my DevOps fundamentals across Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, AWS, and automation.
- Build production-style DevOps projects that improve my practical skills and strengthen my GitHub portfolio.
- Improve my DevOps interview preparation and confidently transition into my next DevOps role.

---

## Core Skills to Build

- Containerization and Container Orchestration (Docker & Kubernetes)
- CI/CD Pipeline Development and Automation
- Python Automation for DevOps

---

## Weekly Time Commitment

- **Weekdays:** 2–3 hours of focused learning and hands-on practice.
- **Weekends:** Additional time for projects, revision, and completing pending tasks.

---

## Consistency Plan

To stay consistent throughout this challenge, I will:

- Complete at least one task from the roadmap every day.
- Document my learning, notes, and projects on GitHub.
- Share my progress and key takeaways on LinkedIn.
- Write technical blogs for topics that are worth exploring in more depth.
- Revise previous topics regularly instead of only focusing on new ones.
- Build projects alongside learning to strengthen practical understanding.
- Continue preparing for DevOps interviews throughout the journey.

---

## What Success Looks Like After 90 Days

By the end of this challenge, I want to have:

- A strong portfolio of DevOps projects.
- Better confidence in production-oriented DevOps concepts and tools.
- Improved interview readiness.
- A consistent record of learning and sharing my progress.
- The skills and confidence to take the next step in my DevOps career.
